# Surgical AI Agents Workshop: Participant Guide

Welcome to the **Surgical AI Agents Workshop**. This guide will help you deconstruct a surgical clinical problem and design an agentic AI research workflow during today's hands-on exercise.

---

## 1. What We Are Building

We are **not** building a chatbot that gives medical advice.

We are designing a **Multi-Agent Evidence Research Assistant**:
- A **Coordinator Agent** that takes a surgical inquiry and breaks it down into research components.
- An **Evidence Specialist** that searches peer-reviewed literature for clinical trials (RCTs) and systematic reviews.
- A **Guideline Specialist** that searches official surgical societies (e.g., ASCRS, ERAS Society, SAGES, EAES, NICE) for clinical practice guidelines.
- A **Structured Brief** that organizes findings, highlights where trials and guidelines agree or conflict, and provides verified source URLs.

---

## 2. What Kind of Surgical Problem to Choose

Choose a surgical problem that involves:
- **Clinical uncertainty or debate** (e.g., two accepted surgical techniques).
- **Novel surgical technology** (e.g., fluorescence imaging, robotic surgery, AI assistance).
- **Perioperative bundles** (e.g., prehabilitation, nutrition, bowel prep, glycemic control).
- **Timing dilemmas** (e.g., early index cholecystectomy vs delayed interval surgery).

---

## 3. What Makes a Good Agent Problem

A good agent problem has clear epidemiological boundaries that can be framed using the **PICOT** model:

| Element | Question to Ask | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P** — Population | Which patient cohort? | Adults undergoing elective colorectal surgery |
| **I** — Intervention | What procedure, protocol, or device? | Multimodal prehabilitation (exercise + nutrition) |
| **C** — Comparator | Compared to what alternative? | Standard perioperative care / unassisted care |
| **O** — Outcomes | Which clinical endpoints matter? | 30-day complications, functional capacity (6MWD), length of stay |
| **T** — Timeframe | What publication window? | Recent 5–10 years |

---

## 4. What NOT to Enter (Strict Safety Rules)

> [!CAUTION]
> **STRICT SAFETY BOUNDARIES**:
> - **DO NOT USE REAL PATIENT DATA**: Never enter medical record numbers, patient initials, clinic notes, or dates of surgery.
> - **DO NOT ENTER PHI**: Protected Health Information is strictly barred from all AI prompts.
> - **DO NOT ASK FOR PATIENT-SPECIFIC DECISIONS**: Do not ask *"Should I operate on patient X on Tuesday?"* or *"What antibiotic dose should I give this 72yo male?"*
> 
> *Our AI system is an academic research assistant for education and literature review. It is NOT a clinical decision support system.*

## 6. How to Interpret the Output

When you inspect an evidence brief generated by the multi-agent system, look for these key sections:

1. **`research_scope`**: Did the Coordinator accurately capture your population, intervention, and endpoints?
2. **`evidence_findings`**: Are the cited studies actual randomized trials or meta-analyses? Look at the study sample sizes and primary endpoints.
3. **`guideline_findings`**: Which societies have weighed in? Is the recommendation strong, conditional, or absent?
4. **`areas_of_agreement`**: Where do trials and guidelines concur?
5. **`areas_of_disagreement`**: Where is there clinical controversy, international variation, or conflicting trial results?
6. **`not_found`**: Did the agent honestly flag missing data instead of inventing fake studies?
7. **`sources`**: Are the URLs verified, clickable links?

---

## 7. How to Turn Your Idea into a Next Step

After the workshop, you can take your structured AI agent design and develop it further:
- **Literature Review / Meta-analysis**: Use the PICOT deconstruction and search parameters as the foundation for a formal PRISMA systematic review protocol.
- **Grant Proposal / Research Protocol**: Use the identified `areas_of_disagreement` and `uncertainty` to formulate an investigator-initiated clinical trial hypothesis.
- **Institutional Quality Improvement (QI)**: Compare your hospital's surgical clinical pathways against the catalog of society guidelines and identify practice gaps.
- **Code Implementation**: Inspect the developer ADK implementation in this repository to see how to deploy this multi-agent system into automated Python scripts or local pipelines.
