# Surgical Research Demo Questions

This document compiles curated surgical research inquiries designed for the 60-minute educational AI agent workshop. Each question illustrates how an overarching clinical research topic can be decomposed into an evidence review task and a clinical guideline analysis task.

All questions are educational and research-oriented. None request patient-specific management or diagnosis.

---

## Primary Demo Question (Colorectal Surgery & Prehabilitation)

### Demo Question 1: Multimodal Prehabilitation in Elective Colorectal Surgery
> **Prompt**:  
> "For adults undergoing elective colorectal surgery, what outcome measures and study designs have recent randomized trials and systematic reviews used to evaluate prehabilitation, and what do major professional-society or national guidelines say about its role and implementation?"
>
> - **Category**: Multi-Domain Synthesis (Outcomes, Comparative Effectiveness & Guideline Comparison)
> - **Target Deconstruction**:
>   - **Population**: Adult patients scheduled for elective colorectal resection.
>   - **Intervention**: Multimodal prehabilitation (exercise, nutritional support, psychological coaching/counseling).
>   - **Comparator**: Standard preoperative care or unimodal interventions.
>   - **Outcomes**: Functional capacity (e.g. 6-minute walk distance), 30-day post-op complications (Clavien-Dindo), hospital length of stay, patient-reported recovery.
>   - **Guideline Targets**: ERAS Society guidelines, ASCRS clinical practice guidelines, NICE guidance.

## Additional Curated Workshop Questions

### Demo Question 2: Minimally Invasive vs. Open Pancreaticoduodenectomy (Comparative Effectiveness)
> **Prompt**:  
> "In patients undergoing pancreaticoduodenectomy for periampullary tumors, how do randomized controlled trials compare minimally invasive (laparoscopic or robotic) approaches against open surgery regarding delayed gastric emptying, postoperative pancreatic fistula (POPF) rates, and oncological margins, and how do international consensus guidelines address learning curve thresholds?"
>
> - **Category**: Comparative Effectiveness & Surgical Safety
> - **Target Deconstruction**:
>   - **Population**: Adults with resectable periampullary or pancreatic head tumors.
>   - **Intervention**: Minimally invasive pancreaticoduodenectomy (MIPD - robotic or laparoscopic).
>   - **Comparator**: Open pancreaticoduodenectomy (OPD).
>   - **Outcomes**: ISGPS Grade B/C POPF, delayed gastric emptying (DGE), R0 resection rates, 90-day mortality.
>   - **Guideline Targets**: Miami / Brescia International Evidence-Based Guidelines on Minimally Invasive Pancreas Resection, NCCN, E-AHPBA consensus statements.

### Demo Question 3: Indocyanine Green (ICG) Fluorescence Angiography for Anastomotic Perfusion (Outcomes & Technology)
> **Prompt**:  
> "What clinical trial evidence exists regarding the impact of intraoperative indocyanine green (ICG) fluorescence angiography on reducing anastomotic leak rates in left-sided colorectal and low anterior resections, and what do surgical society guidelines conclude regarding routine implementation?"
>
> - **Category**: Surgical Technology, Intraoperative Workflow & Outcomes
> - **Target Deconstruction**:
>   - **Population**: Adult patients undergoing left-sided colectomy or anterior resection with colorectal/coloanal anastomosis.
>   - **Intervention**: Intraoperative ICG fluorescence imaging/angiography to evaluate perfusion.
>   - **Comparator**: Subjective visual assessment (tissue color, bleeding margins, pulsation).
>   - **Outcomes**: Clinically relevant anastomotic leak rate, intraoperative revision of transection line, reoperation rate.
>   - **Guideline Targets**: EAES/SAGES consensus statements, ASCRS practice parameters, ESCP guidelines.

### Demo Question 4: External Validation Methodologies for Machine Learning Surgical Risk Scores (AI Research Methodology)
> **Prompt**:  
> "In surgical risk prediction research, what methodologies and reporting standards do current systematic reviews identify for the external validation of machine learning risk prediction models (e.g., mortality or complication prediction), and how do TRIPOD-AI and DECIDE-AI reporting guidelines structure model transportability assessment?"
>
> - **Category**: AI Research Methodology & Validation
> - **Target Deconstruction**:
>   - **Population**: General, gastrointestinal, or cardiac surgical research cohorts.
>   - **Intervention**: Multivariable machine learning risk prediction models.
>   - **Comparator**: Traditional logistic regression scores (e.g. POSSUM, NSQIP surgical risk calculator).
>   - **Outcomes**: Discrimination (C-statistic / AUROC), calibration (calibration slope, Brier score), clinical utility (decision curve analysis).
>   - **Guideline Targets**: TRIPOD-AI statement, DECIDE-AI guideline, EQUATOR Network reporting standards.

### Demo Question 5: Optimal Timing of Cholecystectomy for Mild Acute Biliary Pancreatitis (Surgical Workflow & Timing)
> **Prompt**:  
> "What do randomized controlled trials and contemporary meta-analyses demonstrate regarding index admission (early within 48–72 hours) versus delayed (interval) laparoscopic cholecystectomy for mild acute biliary pancreatitis in terms of recurrent biliary events and readmissions, and what are the specific recommendations from IAP/APA and WSES guidelines?"
>
> - **Category**: Surgical Workflow & Clinical Timing
> - **Target Deconstruction**:
>   - **Population**: Adults presenting with first episode of mild acute gallstone pancreatitis.
>   - **Intervention**: Early index admission cholecystectomy (within 48-72h of symptom resolution).
>   - **Comparator**: Delayed interval cholecystectomy (4–6 weeks post-discharge).
>   - **Outcomes**: Recurrent biliary events (gallstone pancreatitis, cholangitis, colic), surgical complication rate, total hospital stay.
>   - **Guideline Targets**: IAP/APA acute pancreatitis guidelines, WSES guidelines on acute calculous cholecystitis and pancreatitis.

### Demo Question 6: Prophylactic Mesh Placement in Stoma Formation (Guideline Comparison & Synthesis)
> **Prompt**:  
> "What is the consensus among European and American surgical societies regarding the routine use of prophylactic synthetic or biological mesh during elective end colostomy creation to prevent parastomal hernia, and how does this compare with the findings of recent systematic reviews on long-term mesh-related complications?"
>
> - **Category**: Guideline Comparison & Long-Term Outcomes
> - **Target Deconstruction**:
>   - **Population**: Adult patients undergoing elective permanent end colostomy creation (e.g., abdominoperineal resection).
>   - **Intervention**: Prophylactic mesh reinforcement at initial stoma formation (retromuscular, onlay, or sublay).
>   - **Comparator**: Conventional stoma creation without mesh.
>   - **Outcomes**: Parastomal hernia incidence (clinical and radiological), surgical site infection, mesh erosion, stoma revision.
>   - **Guideline Targets**: European Hernia Society (EHS) guidelines, ASCRS clinical practice guidelines.

### Demo Question 7: Perioperative Glycemic Control Targets in Major Abdominal Surgery (Postoperative Recovery)
> **Prompt**:  
> "For diabetic and non-diabetic surgical patients undergoing major abdominal surgery, what do clinical trials show regarding intensive versus moderate perioperative glycemic control targets on surgical site infection rates, and how do CDC and WHO surgical site infection prevention guidelines address blood glucose thresholds?"
>
> - **Category**: Postoperative Recovery & Complication Prevention
> - **Target Deconstruction**:
>   - **Population**: Diabetic and non-diabetic adult patients undergoing major abdominal surgery.
>   - **Intervention**: Strict/intensive perioperative glycemic control (target blood glucose <110–140 mg/dL).
>   - **Comparator**: Conventional or moderate glycemic control (target <180–200 mg/dL).
>   - **Outcomes**: Surgical site infections (SSI), hypoglycemia episodes, 30-day morbidity.
>   - **Guideline Targets**: WHO Global Guidelines for the Prevention of Surgical Site Infection, CDC SSI guidelines, ERAS society consensus.

### Demo Question 8: Computer Vision for Intraoperative Critical View of Safety (AI in Surgery Validation)
> **Prompt**:  
> "What study designs, benchmark video datasets, and validation metrics have researchers utilized to evaluate deep learning algorithms for real-time identification of the Critical View of Safety (CVS) during laparoscopic cholecystectomy, and what formal consensus exists on requirements before clinical deployment?"
>
> - **Category**: AI Research Methodology & Intraoperative Workflow
> - **Target Deconstruction**:
>   - **Population**: Video cohorts of laparoscopic cholecystectomy procedures.
>   - **Intervention**: Deep learning computer vision models detecting anatomical landmarks and CVS criteria.
>   - **Comparator**: Expert surgeon ground-truth annotation.
>   - **Outcomes**: Frame-level and phase-level sensitivity/specificity, mean average precision (mAP), inference latency, bile duct injury risk mitigation.
>   - **Guideline Targets**: SAGES Safe Cholecystectomy Task Force recommendations, IDEAL-AI framework for surgical AI evaluation.
