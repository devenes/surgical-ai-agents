# Multimodal Demonstration Assets & Licensing Registry

- **Directory Scope**: Demonstration imagery for the supplemental multimodal workshop demo.
- **Safety Rule**: **100% Zero-PHI and Zero Patient Identification**.

---

## 1. Compliance & Licensing Standard

In strict compliance with healthcare privacy laws (HIPAA, GDPR) and ethical AI research standards:
- **No Real Patient Imagery**: No intraoperative patient photographs, diagnostic radiological scans (CT, MRI, X-ray), or identifiable surgical videos are permitted in this repository.
- **No Copyright Infringements**: All demonstration imagery must be either:
  1. Procedurally generated synthetic diagrams created natively in code.
  2. Public-domain / Creative Commons Zero (CC0) anatomical diagrams.
  3. Open-access medical illustrations with explicit permissive attribution licenses (CC-BY 4.0).

## 3. Image Description: `laparoscopic_cvs_schematic.png`

The synthetic image depicts an educational vector schematic of a laparoscopic right upper quadrant operative field:
- **Anatomy**: Shows the liver contour, gallbladder fundus/infundibulum, hepatocystic triangle (Calot's triangle), cystic duct, cystic artery, and common bile duct.
- **Dissection Planes**: Highlights the cleared cystic plate and mobilized lower third of the gallbladder.
- **Instruments**: Illustrates a generic laparoscopic atraumatic grasper applying cephalad-lateral traction.
- **Purpose**: Provides clean, non-patient, non-copyrighted geometric features for testing vision-language model observation and spatial recognition without clinical or privacy liabilities.
