# Synthetic Surgical Research Scenarios

## Educational Scope & Privacy Rules

The scenarios in this directory are **100% synthetic research personas and vignettes** designed strictly for educational workshops and agent evaluation.

### Mandatory Compliance Rules
1. **Zero Real Patient Data**: No actual patient records, real patient histories, or clinical records are permitted.
2. **Zero Protected Health Information (PHI)**: No names, real dates, institutional medical record numbers (MRNs), or geographic identifiers.
3. **Research-Question Framing**: Scenarios represent generalized clinical archetypes (e.g., "A research cohort evaluating elderly patients undergoing laparoscopic colectomy") rather than individual patient decision queries.
4. **Safety Verification**: If any prompt in this directory is submitted to the system, the agents must treat it as an educational research archetype and must never provide individualized medical guidance.
