# Safety & Clinical Boundaries

## Project Nature & Mission

The **Surgical AI Agents** system is an **educational and research proof-of-concept (POC)** developed specifically for a 60-minute interactive workshop for surgeons. 

The system demonstrates how multi-agent architectures (Coordinator, Evidence Agent, Guideline Agent) can decompose complex surgical research queries, ground answers in peer-reviewed scientific literature and clinical guidelines, and output structured evidence summaries.

---

## Strict Prohibitions & Hard Boundaries

This repository and all agents operating within it are bound by the following non-negotiable safety rules:

### 1. No Real Patient Data & Zero PHI
- The system must never ingest, process, store, or output Protected Health Information (PHI) or Personally Identifiable Information (PII).
- Real patient hospital records, operative notes, laboratory printouts, imaging files, pathology reports, or identifying details are strictly prohibited.
- All testing, benchmarking, and demonstration materials must utilize **100% synthetic, public, or generalized research inquiries**.

### 2. No Patient-Specific Clinical Advice or Decisions
- The system must never advise on the management of an individual patient (e.g., answering *"Should I operate on patient X today?"* or *"What dose of antibiotic should I administer to this patient?"*).
- If a user submits a clinical vignette with patient-specific parameters, the system must trigger an automatic safety boundary refusal:
  - Decline to provide individualized clinical advice.
  - Remind the user that clinical care requires licensed human medical judgment.
  - Reframe the topic as a generalized surgical research question.

### 3. No Diagnosis & No Clinical Decision Support Claims
- The system does not diagnose diseases or conditions.
- The system is **NOT** a Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) and makes no claims to be a medical device under FDA, EU MDR, or international medical software regulations.

### 4. Zero Tolerance for Hallucinated Evidence or Citations
- Surgical practice depends on evidence integrity. The agents must never fabricate paper titles, author names, journal names, DOIs, publication years, or URLs.
- Grounding via Google Search and URL context must be verified before citing.
- If grounding is absent or inconclusive, the agent must decline to cite rather than produce a plausible hallucination.

### 5. Mandatory Epistemic Humility (Uncertainty & "Not Found")
- When scientific consensus is lacking or trial results conflict, the system must explicitly document the conflict under `areas_of_disagreement` and `uncertainty`.
- When reliable peer-reviewed literature or formal society guidelines cannot be identified, the system must explicitly report `not_found: true` rather than extrapolate or guess.
- Uncertainty must never be converted into a recommendation.

---

## Mandatory Safety Disclaimer

Every generated brief and user-facing response must conclude with the standard safety disclaimer:

> **Educational & Research Safety Disclaimer**:  
> *This evidence brief was generated by an educational AI research prototype for instructional and academic review purposes only. It is not intended for clinical diagnosis, patient-specific treatment recommendations, or as a clinical decision support system. Any patient management decisions must be made independently by licensed healthcare professionals based on individual clinical evaluation.*

## Safety Checklist for Future Development

Before deploying updates or moving to future phases, complete this audit checklist:

- [ ] **Prompt Guardrails**: Are system prompts enforcing the refusal of patient-specific management queries?
- [ ] **Data Hygiene**: Has the repository been scanned for accidental PHI, test medical records, or API credentials?
- [ ] **Schema Enforcement**: Does the output schema strictly mandate `not_found` flags and the `safety_disclaimer`?
- [ ] **Grounding Verification**: Are all specialist agents constrained to output verified citations?
- [ ] **Synthetic Scenarios**: Are all workshop test cases confirmed to be synthetic archetypes?
- [ ] **Boundary Audit**: Did the test suite run with zero failures on `EDGE-07-PATIENT-SPECIFIC-SAFETY`?
